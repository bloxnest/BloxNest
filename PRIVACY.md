# BloxNest privacy

BloxNest doesn't collect, store or send any personal data. No accounts, passwords, cookies, analytics or tracking.

**What it connects to.** Only Roblox's own server (`clientsettingscdn.roblox.com`), to check which Roblox version is newest. It does this when you press Start (if "Update Roblox before opening" is on) and every 30 minutes while it's running (if "Tell me when Roblox updates" is on). Nothing about you is sent, beyond what any normal web request carries.

**Updates.** BloxNest doesn't download any files. If your Roblox is out of date, it runs the Roblox updater that's already on your PC, after checking that the updater is signed by Roblox Corporation.

**Roblox itself.** BloxNest doesn't modify Roblox's files or inject anything into the game. It opens Roblox normally and stops Roblox's "only one window" check from closing the extra windows. When you press Start while Roblox is open, it closes your open Roblox windows first (after you confirm).

**Files on your PC.** The only file BloxNest saves is your options: `%AppData%\BloxNest\settings.ini`. Delete it to reset them.
